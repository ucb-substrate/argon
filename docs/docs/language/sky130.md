# SKY130 library

`pdks/sky130` holds the SKY130 technology file and an Argon library of layout cells for it. A library uses both through its [manifest](/language/modules-manifests#library-manifest), with paths relative to the manifest:

```toml
name = "mylib"
tech = "../argon/pdks/sky130/sky130.tech.toml"

[dependencies]
sky130 = "../argon/pdks/sky130"
```

Then import its items by name, as in `use sky130::{mos, tap};`. Lengths are in nm, on a 5 nm grid.

## Layers

A layer's name is the layer and its purpose, as in `met1.drawing`; a bare `met1` isn't defined. These are the layers a transistor-level layout draws. The technology file defines the rest.

| Layer | GDS | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nwell.drawing` | 64/20 | N-well, under PMOS devices and n-taps |
| `diff.drawing` | 65/20 | Transistor diffusion |
| `tap.drawing` | 65/44 | Well and substrate tap diffusion |
| `poly.drawing` | 66/20 | Gates |
| `npc.drawing` | 95/20 | Nitride cut, around every poly contact |
| `nsdm.drawing`, `psdm.drawing` | 93/44, 94/20 | N+ implant over NMOS diffusion and n-taps; P+ over PMOS diffusion and p-taps |
| `lvtn.drawing`, `hvtp.drawing` | 125/44, 78/44 | Low-threshold NMOS and high-threshold PMOS implants |
| `licon1.drawing` | 66/44 | Contacts from diffusion, taps, and poly to li1 |
| `li1.drawing` | 67/20 | Local interconnect |
| `mcon.drawing` | 67/44 | Contacts from li1 to met1 |
| `met1.drawing` to `met5.drawing` | 68/20 to 72/20 | Metals 1 to 5 |
| `via.drawing` | 68/44 | Vias from met1 to met2 |
| `via2.drawing`, `via3.drawing`, `via4.drawing` | 69/44, 70/44, 71/44 | Vias from met2 to met3, met3 to met4, and met4 to met5 |
| `capm.drawing` | 89/44 | MIM capacitor top plate, over a met3 bottom plate and contacted with via3 |

`li1` and each metal also have a `.pin` layer (datatype 16) and a `.label` layer (datatype 5). A port is a shape on a pin layer over the net's shape on the matching drawing layer, with the port's name as text on the label layer inside it:

```argon
let p = rect("met1.pin", w=140., h=140.);
center_rects(bar, p);
text("vdd", "met1.label", p.x0 + 70., p.y0 + 70.);
```

## Rules and vias

The technology file's `[rules]` table holds SKY130's public periphery rules, read with [`rule`](/language/std-layout#rule), such as `rule("met1.space")` or `rule("diff.licon_end_enclosure")`; each line's comment names the rule a DRC report would cite. Its `[[vias]]` entries let [`via`](/language/std-layout#via) contact poly, diffusion, or a tap to li1, and each metal to the next, and let [`via_stack`](/language/std-layout#via-stack) join any two of them. A MIM capacitor's top plate, `capm.drawing`, takes `via3` up to met4.

## Cells

`lib.ar` documents each cell and function, and the [quick reference](/language/reference#sky130-library) lists them.

- `fet1v8(nfet, w, nf)` draws a transistor's diffusion, its `nf` gate fingers, 150 wide on a 430 pitch, and an li1 contact column beside each finger. The diffusion ends 60 past the outer columns. It leaves the gates unjoined and the columns unstrapped.
- `mos(nfet, w, nf)` wires a `fet1v8` on met1: a gate bar `g` below the diffusion, or above it when placed with `reflect=true`, and bars `s` and `d` over alternate contact columns. Each bar is 320 tall, which fits a via1 with its enclosure. Keywords change the wiring:
  - `swap=true` puts `d` on the gate side and `s` on the far side, and `away=true` moves the gate-side bar next to the far one, which leaves the gate side free of met1. Abutted devices need the same `away` unless `w` is at least 860.
  - `gate_side` widens a lone finger's 270-wide gate contact to one side only, and `gate_row` moves the contact 540 further from the diffusion per row.
  - `straps=false` leaves `s` and `d` undrawn.
- `abut(devices)` places `mos` or `cmos` instances side by side so that neighbors share a contact column. Mirror one with `reflect=true, angle=180` to choose which of its columns is shared. Lone fingers with their gates on the same side fit in one row only when, as placed, the left one's contact widens left and the right one's right (`gate_side`). Otherwise alternate `gate_row`s, with the contact nearer the diffusion not widening toward its neighbors.
- `cmos(nw, pw, nf)` draws an NMOS and a PMOS that share their gate fingers, as in an inverter, with the gate contacted between them and the drains strapped on either side of it. Columns abut into one row of shared diffusion, and `rows` and `gate_row` give neighbors with different gates separate contacts.
- `fold(nfet, w, nf, rows)` splits one transistor into `rows` stacked rows of `mos`, joined by met2 trunks `g`, `s`, and `d`.
- `tap(ntap, w, h)` draws a tap with li1 over it and as many contacts as fit, an n-tap if `ntap` is `true`. Contacts fit only when `w` is at least 250 and `h` at least 410.
- `tap_m1(ntap, w, h)` adds met1 over the tap's outline, contacted to its li1, so a narrow, tall one is a tap column at the end of a row. `fill_tap(ntap, span)` places one whose implant fills a rectangle.
- `inv` is a complete inverter that shows `fet1v8` and `tap` in use.

Two layout choices the cells leave to you matter for area:
- A tap column at a row's end costs about 520 of width at that end, 270 of spacing to the diffusion and a 250-wide tap. A full-width tap rail costs its height and spacing across the whole row. Columns are cheaper unless the cell is much taller than wide.
- Devices that share a source or drain net share diffusion when they abut. Every separate diffusion costs at least 270 of spacing plus 60 of diffusion at each end.
