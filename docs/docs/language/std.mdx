## `std::intersection` {#intersection}

<ApiItem id="intersection" name="std::intersection" signature={'std::intersection(rect1: Rect, rect2: Rect) -> Rect'} summary={<>The overlap of two rectangles, as a construction rectangle.</>}>

<ParameterTable rows={[
  {name: 'rect1', type: 'Rect', typeHref: '/language/types/rect', requirement: 'Required', description: 'First rectangle.'},
  {name: 'rect2', type: 'Rect', typeHref: '/language/types/rect', requirement: 'Required', description: 'Second rectangle.'},
]} />

<Returns type="Rect" href="/language/types/rect">A construction rectangle whose lower edges are the larger of the two inputs' and whose upper edges are the smaller.</Returns>

If the rectangles don't overlap, the result is inverted rather than empty: this computes edge expressions and doesn't test for overlap.

</ApiItem>
